    Binary search tree rules

    • Every key in the left subtree is smaller than the node. Every key in the right subtree is larger.
    • Search and insert follow one path from the root, so the cost is the height of the tree.
    • Delete has three cases: leaf (remove it), one child (the child takes its place), two children (copy the inorder successor, the smallest key in the right subtree, then remove that successor).
    • Inorder traversal of a BST always gives sorted order.

    AVL tree rules

    • Balance factor = height of left subtree minus height of right subtree.
    • A node is balanced when its balance factor is -1, 0 or 1. Outside that range the tree rotates.
    • Inserting in ascending order turns a plain BST into a chain with height n. An AVL tree stays near log n.
