How scoring works

  • Each correct answer earns XP: 100 (easy), 150 (medium), 200 (hard) or 300 (boss).
  • Answer quickly for up to 50 bonus XP. You have 25 seconds per question.
  • Every correct answer in a row raises your multiplier by 0.25, up to x2. A wrong answer resets it.
  • A hint removes two wrong options but costs 30 XP.
  • Collect badges, then check your accuracy for each course outcome (CO) at the end.
